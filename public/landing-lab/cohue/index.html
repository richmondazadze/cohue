How it happens

From “we should”
to “let’s start.”

One connected process, from the first conversation to a shared place to work. Clear enough to follow. Flexible enough for real people.

  1. 01 / INTENT

    Say it.

    A repair café. A prototype. A project you keep putting off. Talk it through with the Coordinator by voice or text, then review the brief before discovery begins.

  2. 02 / FIT

    Find them.

    COHUE looks for opted-in people whose confirmed skills and availability fit. Review the proposed group, coverage and shared time. A match is a possibility, never a commitment.

  3. 03 / CONSENT

    A real yes.

    Each person reads their role, responsibilities, schedule and group. They accept, decline or suggest a change. Original terms stay visible beside reviewed translations and optional audio.

  4. 04 / REPAIR

    Keep going.

    If someone declines, COHUE makes the gap visible and looks for a valid repair. If the commitments or group change, affected people decide again. Nothing is accepted on their behalf.

  5. 05 / ACTION

    Make it real.

    Once the required people agree, open the persistent Mission Room: approved roles, first tasks, original and localized briefs, and an advisory kickoff plan for the first hour.

A few clear answers

Know what
you are joining.

Good collaboration starts with clear expectations. Here is what to know before your first mission.

Do I need an idea to join?

No. Share the skills you want to offer and when you are available. Opt in to being proposed for missions, then decide on each invitation.

Does AI make commitments for me?

No. The Coordinator helps clarify and coordinate. Each participant decides on their own current invitation terms before activation.

What happens if someone says no?

COHUE looks for a feasible repair. A changed group or commitment requires a new decision from affected people.

Can I explore without involving real people?

Yes. Choose the clearly labeled synthetic showcase from the app home. Its people are synthetic; matching, commands and state changes use the real workflow.

Who can see my mission?

The creator sees the Theater. An invitation link grants access to that invitation. The Room is for the creator and authorized activated members. Keep personal invitation links private.

What is a kickoff plan?

An advisory starting point for approved roles. It helps the team begin, but never replaces the agreed terms or assigned tasks.
