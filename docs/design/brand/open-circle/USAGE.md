# COHUE — Open Circle usage guide v1

Direction A selected by the user on 24 October 2026. Digital identity files are prepared for review/use; this selection does not approve the entire UI system or authorize application implementation.

The open C makes room for one more person. Its separate square preserves individual agency. Use the horizontal signature in headers, stacked signature on spacious title surfaces, and symbol alone for compact navigation/app icons. The custom COHUE letterforms are filled vector paths, not a font. Do not reconstruct the name by typing it.

Keep **one square-height of clear space** outside the visible artwork (38 units on the standard 256-unit symbol canvas). File padding is not always the complete clear-space zone. Keep the symbol and wordmark proportions/gap fixed. Digital minimums: symbol 24px using standard drawing; 16–23px use the small-size cut; horizontal signature 120px wide; stacked 112px wide. Prefer 160px horizontal in spacious headers. These are inspected screen recommendations; print minimums are provisional: symbol 7mm, horizontal 32mm, subject to physical proof.

Use blue or ink on white/warm context, black on white, or white on blue/ink. Use optically adjusted reversed symbol/signature masters when available; the standard automatic white exports remain useful for comparing unchanged geometry. Keep the logo independent of mission status colors: a green approval does not recolor the identity. On busy images place the mark on a calm white or ink plate with full clear space. No gradients, shadows, stretch, rotation, closing the aperture or moving the square into the ring. App tiles use a blue background with a white small-size symbol; maskable icon has reserved safe area.

Interface typography remains proposed Space Grotesk 400–700, SIL OFL 1.1, with original notices and source hashes in `../../proposal-v1/fonts/`. The custom logo paths do not depend on that font. Brand voice: warm, direct, specific. Tagline: “From strangers to something extraordinary.” Keep the tagline as ordinary text outside the fixed signature, minimum 16px; never force it into a tiny favicon.

The aperture and rounded square can inform selection outlines and simple supporting layouts. Avoid filling the app with repeated logos. The graph's person/requirement/resource geometry retains its separate semantic purpose. Static header mark is the default; optional one-time ≤180ms opacity entrance only, no looping spin/pulse and no apparent agent progress. Reduced motion renders immediately. No sonic signature/autoplay is proposed.

Files: `masters/` editable SVG source; `digital/` monochrome/blue/ink SVGs, transparent PNGs, web icon set and deployment snippets; `presentation.html` digital context studies. `PACKAGE.md` lists contents and verification limits. No PDF/AI/EPS or print-color proof is claimed. The identity artwork is original geometric work; references/library marks were not traced. Trademark and reverse-image clearance have not been performed.

Master record: this directory in the COHUE repository; changes reviewed with the user/frontend owner. Do not invent legal trademark registration marks. Keep a copy of the selected source with each future export revision.
