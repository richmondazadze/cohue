01

Entry

A clear ambition, a safe network excerpt, Start or Join.

02

Onboarding

Skills, needs, language, time and separate visibility choices.

03

Composer

Speak or type. Correct intent. Confirm requirements.

04

Theater

Inspect real work, feasible proposals and individual responses.

05

Invitation

Readable localized terms. Accept, decline or counter.

07
